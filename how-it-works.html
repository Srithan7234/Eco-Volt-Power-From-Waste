The Science Behind the Power

What is an MFC?

Bacteria in greywater break down organic matter → release electrons → captured by carbon felt electrodes → stored as electricity.

Turbocharging Explained

Aeration increases oxygen at cathode → boosts voltage output.

Dual Benefit

Purifies water (BOD, turbidity drop)

Generates low-voltage electricity (~4.5–8.5W/day)
